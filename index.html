About Me

I am Mario Villa, a lifestyle creator and digital marketing specialist based in Scottsdale, Arizona. I have been creating content since 2016, building a combined audience of more than 90K followers across Instagram and TikTok with content covering travel, hotels, fashion, and everyday life.

Before creating full time, I spent six years in B2B digital marketing at Quisitive running paid search and paid social campaigns, with earlier experience at Collab, SoulPancake, Thoughtful Media Group, and AwesomenessTV. That combination of creator instinct and marketing discipline shapes every brand partnership I take on.

My brand work includes Giorgio Armani, Abercrombie and Fitch, Valentino, GEICO, Tiff's Treats, and Simon Malls, plus hotel partnerships with Hyatt, Canopy by Hilton, Fairmont, Hotel Glorieta, and Postcard Cabins. I am currently pursuing an M.S. in AI in Business at Arizona State University, studying where content creation and AI meet.
