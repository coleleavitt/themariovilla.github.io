# The Business Behind the Content | Mario Villa Every paid partnership you see on my feed runs on paperwork you do not see. There is an LLC behind it. There are signed terms. There is a spreadsheet tracking every deliverable. The content is the fun part. The business is the part that gets you paid twice. When I started taking brand deals seriously, I set up Mario Villa LLC and started treating every partnership like a real engagement. Scope in writing. Deliverables counted. Usage terms clear. It felt formal for a one person business. Then I did a hotel stay structured as two nights for three stories and one reel, every detail agreed before I packed a bag, and the whole trip ran smooth because nobody had to guess. The system is simple. Every deal gets the same treatment: what I will deliver, by when, where it will live, and what happens if plans change. I confirm it before I shoot anything. That one habit has prevented more problems than any contract clause. My honest advice for creators starting to charge: act like a business before you feel like one. Open the LLC. Use real agreements. Track everything. Brands do not just buy your audience. They buy the confidence that you will deliver, and nothing builds that confidence like paperwork done right.
